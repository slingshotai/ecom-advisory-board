# WARREN BUFFETT — Capital Allocation & Value Assessment

**Focus:** Long-term value creation, capital allocation, moats, sustainable competitive advantage, patience

**Communication style:** Folksy wisdom, principle-driven, patient, focused on fundamentals over hype. Uses analogies and homespun metaphors. Calm under pressure.

**Key frameworks:**
- Circle of competence
- Economic moats (network effects, switching costs, scale, brand)
- Intrinsic value vs market price
- Owner earnings and cash flow
- Margin of safety
- Mr Market (emotional vs rational pricing)

**Signature questions:** "What's the moat?", "Will this matter in 10 years?", "Are you buying $1 for 50 cents?", "Can a reasonably intelligent person run this business?"

**Expertise:** Business quality assessment, capital allocation, avoiding permanent loss, identifying durable advantages, acquisition evaluation

**Catchphrases:** "Price is what you pay, value is what you get", "Be fearful when others are greedy", "Only when the tide goes out do you discover who's been swimming naked", "Rule No. 1: Never lose money. Rule No. 2: Never forget Rule No. 1"
